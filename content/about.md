I'm a Lay Pastor with the Canadian Baptists of Ontario and Quebec (CBOQ) and a Certified AWS and Microsoft Azure Solutions Architect with over 20 years of experience in Information Technology. In my dual role, I serve both the spiritual and technological needs of our community, driving digital transformation in the workplace while guiding others in their faith journey.

Currently, I serve as a Government of Canada IT Senior Strategist (IT-05) at the Treasury Board Secretariat, seconded to Employment and Social Development Canada, where I'm transforming their cloud operating model to support initiatives like Benefit Delivery Modernization. Previously, I spent nearly six years with the Canada Revenue Agency's Cloud Centre of Excellence, leading enterprise-wide cloud adoption strategies and modernizing hybrid IT architectures. Before that, I owned and operated a successful web hosting and technical support business for a decade, delivering strategic solutions to diverse industries.

My career is defined by a passion for innovation, a knack for solving complex problems, and a commitment to empowering teams and organizations through technical leadership and clear communication. As a Lay Pastor, I bring this same dedication to serving our faith community, helping others navigate their spiritual journey while leveraging technology to enhance our ministry's reach and impact.

* Expertise: Cloud architecture (AWS, Azure), digital transformation, strategic IT planning, hybrid IT modernization, and pastoral care.

* Leadership: Proven track record guiding executives, technical teams, and faith communities through complex cloud migrations, innovation initiatives, and spiritual growth.

* Impact: Architected solutions handling millions of citizen interactions, from CERB contact centers to CRA's first Protected B cloud environment, while fostering spiritual growth and community connection.

* Passions: Exploring container platforms like OpenShift, mentoring the next generation of IT pros, creating flight simulator tutorials on YouTube (some with over 800,000 views!), and sharing God's love through pastoral ministry.

* Fun Fact: I wrote a keto cookbook - because who says you can't enjoy bacon while staying healthy? And yes, I believe in finding joy in both technology and faith!
