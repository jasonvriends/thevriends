## Scripture: [Acts 2:1-14 MSG](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts%202%3A1-14&version=MSG)

<sup>2 1-4</sup> When the Feast of Pentecost came, they were all together in one place. Without warning there was a sound like a strong wind, gale force—no one could tell where it came from. It filled the whole building. Then, like a wildfire, the Holy Spirit spread through their ranks, and they started speaking in a number of different languages as the Spirit prompted them.

<sup>5-11</sup> There were many Jews staying in Jerusalem just then, devout pilgrims from all over the world. When they heard the sound, they came on the run. Then when they heard, one after another, their own mother tongues being spoken, they were blown away. They couldn't for the life of them figure out what was going on, and kept saying, "Aren't these all Galileans? How come we're hearing them talk in our various mother tongues?

Parthians, Medes, and Elamites;
Visitors from Mesopotamia, Judea, and Cappadocia,
    Pontus and Asia, Phrygia and Pamphylia,
    Egypt and the parts of Libya belonging to Cyrene;
Immigrants from Rome, both Jews and proselytes;
Even Cretans and Arabs!

"They're speaking our languages, describing God's mighty works!"

<sup>12</sup> Their heads were spinning; they couldn't make head or tail of any of it. They talked back and forth, confused: "What's going on here?"

<sup>13</sup> Others joked, "They're drunk on cheap wine."

Peter Speaks Up
<sup>14-21</sup> That's when Peter stood up and, backed by the other eleven, spoke out with bold urgency: "Fellow Jews, all of you who are visiting Jerusalem, listen carefully and get this story straight."

## Transcript

Fellow Jews, who are visiting Jerusalem from all over, listen carefully, and get this story straight. My name is Simon, but you can call me Peter, since that's the nickname Jesus gave me. You remember Jesus, don't you? He was that man you crucified just 50 days ago. He had a sign above his head saying, "Jesus of Nazareth, The King of the Jews."

And yet here you are, to celebrate the Feast of Weeks, with your first fruits of the wheat harvest. You have your two loaves of leavened bread: one in each hand, and are waiting in line for the temple priest, to offer them to God as a wave offering. Well, I ask you this? Why give your offerings to the temple priest, when you could have presented them directly to God? That's right, the man you crucified, Jesus, is the Son of God, our High Prest, our King, our Messiah, and our Savior!

Well, don't feel bad, friends. I was personally chosen by Jesus. He said follow me, and I will make you a fisher of men. I walked alongside Him, had deep conversations with Him, and witnessed His many miracles, and yet I still denied knowing Him, three times before you crucified Him. You see, I lacked the ability, I lacked the boldness, and I lacked the power of the Holy Spirit. But that all changed… just moments ago.

While you were waiting in line, we followers of Jesus, gathered to pray and wait, like Jesus told us to do. Then it happened, gale force wind came out of nowhere. I tell you, if your beards were not connected to your face, they would of flew away in the wind. Each of you gave up your place in line, and rushed over here to witness this event. That itself is a miracle. Giving up your place in line.

But the real miracle, is that we started to praise God in different languages. And yet, each of you heard us praising God in your own native language. Rather than recognize this as a gift from God? (Joel 2:28 NLT)?

Friends, what you've seen, was God's second gift to mankind. God's first gift was Jesus Christ, who you crucified! Now you are witnessing His second gift, the Holy Spirit. That's right! Our God loves you so much, that even after what you did to His son, he gave us another gift. You see, after Jesus was crucified, we followers of Jesus were lost, defeated, we wondered if the last 3 years were wasted time. We asked ourselves how could our Messiah die?
