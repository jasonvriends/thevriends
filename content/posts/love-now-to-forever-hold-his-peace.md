## Scripture: [1 CORINTHIANS 13 (NASB)](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20CORINTHIANS%2013&version=NASB)

<sup>1</sup> If I speak with the tongues of mankind and of angels, but do not have love, I have become a noisy gong or a clanging cymbal. <sup>2</sup> If I have the gift of prophecy and know all mysteries and all knowledge, and if I have all faith so as to remove mountains, but do not have love, I am nothing. <sup>3</sup> And if I give away all my possessions to charity, and if I surrender my body so that I may glory, but do not have love, it does me no good.

<sup>4</sup> Love is patient, love is kind, it is not jealous; love does not brag, it is not arrogant. <sup>5</sup> It does not act disgracefully, it does not seek its own benefit; it is not provoked, does not keep an account of a wrong suffered, <sup>6</sup> it does not rejoice in unrighteousness, but rejoices with the truth; <sup>7</sup> it keeps every confidence, it believes all things, hopes all things, endures all things.

<sup>8</sup> Love never fails; but if there are gifts of prophecy, they will be done away with; if there are tongues, they will cease; if there is knowledge, it will be done away with. <sup>9</sup> For we know in part and prophesy in part; <sup>10</sup> but when the perfect comes, the partial will be done away with. <sup>11</sup> When I was a child, I used to speak like a child, think like a child, reason like a child; when I became a man, I did away with childish things. <sup>12</sup> For now we see in a mirror dimly, but then face to face; now I know in part, but then I will know fully, just as I also have been fully known. <sup>13</sup> But now faith, hope, and love remain, these three; but the greatest of these is love.
